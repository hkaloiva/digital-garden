# Welcome

This is my digital garden — a public collection of notes I'm cultivating over time. Unlike a blog, there's no chronology here. Notes grow, get linked, and get revised as my thinking changes.

Start with one of these maps of content:

## How to read this garden

- Every note links to other notes. Follow the links.
- Notes are never "finished" — they're drafts that get better.
- If something's wrong or incomplete, that's the point. Gardens are messy.
