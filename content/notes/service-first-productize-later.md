# Service first, productize later

My sequence for any new business: **service first, productize later.** Not the other way around.

## The sequence

1. Deliver the service by hand. Learn the real problem, not the assumed one.
2. Notice what repeats. That's the product waiting to be born.
3. Productize the repeatable part. Keep the judgment.
4. Only then consider software.

## Why not product first

- A product built before the service is a guess with a price tag.
- I don't have a big budget (<$5K) or a big team. I can't afford guesses.
- Service revenue funds the product. The product doesn't fund the service.

## What this looks like now

Knowledge audits by hand for a few clients. Then templates and checklists from what actually repeats. Then, maybe, a tool. See [[Knowledge management as a service]].

## The trap

Staying in service forever because it's comfortable and billed hourly. Service first is a phase, not a destination. The line: when 80% of the work is repeatable, productize.
