# The hamster wheel

The enemy isn't failure. The enemy is the hamster wheel — being limited without choice, running hard and going nowhere, with no exit in sight.

## What it looks like

- A role I can't leave because everything depends on me.
- Promises kept so religiously that I become the system.
- Competence that traps me: "only you can do this" becomes "only you do this."
- Stress chosen for the interesting, but never converted into freedom.

## Why it's worse than failure

Failure is at least honest — you know where you stand. The hamster wheel disguises itself as success: busy, needed, paid. The cost shows up later, in the people I said I'd share freedom with.

## The exit

- Build systems around relationships, not just effort.
- Share the load so the client doesn't need me in every conversation.
- Convert service into product so revenue isn't tied to my hours.
- Keep a buffer so a bad month doesn't force a bad decision.
