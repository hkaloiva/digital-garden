# Tight-loose-tight

My operating style in three words: **tight-loose-tight.**

## What it means

- **Tight at the start:** define the outcome, the deadline, the owner, and what "done" looks like. No ambiguity.
- **Loose in the middle:** let people work. Don't hover. Trust the plan and the person.
- **Tight at the end:** verify, close the loop, confirm the promise was kept. No silent endings.

## Why this shape

- Tight-loose-loose fails: good start, no finish, promises leak.
- Tight-tight-tight micromanages: people stop thinking, I become the bottleneck.
- Loose-loose-tight is too late: the damage is done before I notice.

## Where it shows up

- Client projects: scope and SLA agreed up front; execution delegated; review and sign-off at the end.
- My own work: plan the note, write freely, edit ruthlessly.
- Family: clear expectations, then space, then check-in.

## The trap

The middle "loose" can become neglect if I'm not paying attention. Loose means trust, not absence. I still need to be available without taking over.
