# Buffer for transitions

When I moved to England in 2015, I kept money aside for the first few months. That buffer bought me the room to find the right job instead of the first job. I've never forgotten it.

## The principle

A buffer is not savings for their own sake. It's **optionality**. It's the distance between "I have to" and "I choose to."

## Where it applies

- Career moves: leaving a role, starting a business, taking a pay cut for learning.
- Client work: a bad month shouldn't force a rushed decision or a desperate discount.
- Personal life: emergencies happen; panic is expensive.

## How much

Enough to make the next decision on merit, not fear. For me that's a few months of runway, kept liquid and boring. Not invested, not optimised, just there.

## The trap

A buffer can become hoarding — never spending it, never moving. The buffer exists to be used when the moment comes. If I never use it, I've just built a more comfortable cage.
