# Reading to the kids

Every evening: hug them and read them a bedtime story. Non-negotiable. This is a promise I don't break — and the smallest, most important unit of the whole system.

## Why it's non-negotiable

- It's the one part of the day that is purely theirs. Not the client's, not the inbox's.
- It's a kept promise in miniature — small, daily, cumulative. If [[A kept promise is success]] means anything, it means this.
- It grounds the rest of the work. If the ritual is at risk, something upstream is wrong.

## What it protects against

- The slow drift where work eats the evening and "just this once" becomes the norm.
- The illusion that being needed at work is the same as being present at home.
- Burnout disguised as dedication.

## The discipline

- Hard stop in the evening. The inbox will still be there tomorrow.
- If a deadline genuinely threatens it, name the slip early and renegotiate — don't silently sacrifice it.
- Treat it like an SLA with the most important client I have.

## The honest part

At home, work isn't interesting to me — the household commitments hang. I'm aware of it. The ritual is the floor I've chosen not to drop below, even on the days I'd rather not. See [[Household is not my strength]].
