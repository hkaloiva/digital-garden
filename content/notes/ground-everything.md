# Ground everything

Ground everything. No invented numbers, names, or dates. If I don't know, I say "not found" and go find out.

## The rule

- Every claim gets a source, a calculation, or a test.
- No evidence → "not found," not a plausible-sounding answer.
- Estimates are labelled as estimates. Guesses are labelled as guesses.

## Why it matters in service delivery

A client trusts me with their infrastructure. A confident wrong number is worse than an honest "I don't know yet." Trust is built on accuracy, not on sounding certain.

## Why it matters in AI work

AI models hallucinate confidently. If I don't ground my own thinking, I can't tell when the model is doing the same thing. Grounding is the skill that makes me useful next to a machine, not replaced by one.

## The discipline

- Check before stating. Read the doc, run the query, open the file.
- Separate "verified" from "assumed" in every summary.
- When I'm wrong, say so immediately. A corrected promise is still a kept promise.
