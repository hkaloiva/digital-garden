# Don't become an egocentric blowhard

My anti-goal: becoming selfish, an egocentric blowhard. I know the type — "yes, I have a colleague who is like that."

## What this means for me

- Don't turn my competence into arrogance.
- Don't dismiss other people's concerns, even small ones. Tact first.
- Don't let success make me stop listening.
- Credit the team. Be the connector, not the hero.

## The related enemy

The "hamster wheel" — being limited without choice. The opposite of freedom isn't failure; it's **being trapped in a role I can't leave**. Freedom means sharing it with friends, family, and my children.
