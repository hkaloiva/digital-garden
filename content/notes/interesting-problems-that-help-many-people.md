# Interesting problems that help many people

My north star filter for deciding what to work on: **is it an interesting problem, and does it help many people?**

## Why this filter

- "Interesting" keeps me engaged — I burn out on boring work.
- "Helps many people" keeps it meaningful — I don't want to optimize ad clicks.
- The intersection is where I want to build.

## Where it points me

- Service delivery + knowledge management + AI = a problem I find genuinely interesting, affecting thousands of support teams.
- Not: faceless SaaS, volume plays, things that strip out the human.

## The gap

I say I want to start a business but "I don't have a clear goal and I'm not a businessman." The filter gives direction; the next step is picking one problem and going deep. See [[The Dealmaker archetype]].
