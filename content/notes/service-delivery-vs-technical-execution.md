# Service delivery vs technical execution

Two different jobs that look similar from the outside.

## Service delivery

- Owning the client relationship and outcomes
- Communication, expectations, feedback
- Coordinating people and resources
- Saying no / drawing lines

## Technical execution

- Building, configuring, fixing
- Deep problem-solving
- Staying current on tools and platforms

## Why the distinction matters

I'm good at both, but my role is **delivery**. When I drift into technical execution (because it's tempting and I'm fast), I'm spending expensive time on cheap work and starving the team of growth. See [[Drawing the line between delivery and technical work]].
