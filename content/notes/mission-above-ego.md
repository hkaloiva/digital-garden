# Mission above ego

The mission above the ego. Always. The work is in service of the client's goal and the problem being solved — not my status, not my need to be right, not my need to be needed.

## What it costs me

- Credit. The team gets it; I'm the connector.
- Being the hero. Sometimes the right move is invisible.
- Being right. If the client's goal is better served by someone else's idea, that's the idea.

## Why it's hard

Ego is sneaky. It dresses up as standards ("I just care about quality") or as diligence ("I'll do it myself to be sure"). The check: am I protecting the outcome, or protecting my image of myself?

## The Dealmaker risk

A Dealmaker's leverage is presence and trust. That's close to ego's territory. The guardrail: the relationship serves the mission, not the other way around. If I'm the deal but the deal isn't serving the client, I've drifted.
