# Content-first distribution

Build authority before building product. Write about the problem before selling the solution.

## The logic

- Nobody buys from someone they've never heard of.
- Content is the cheapest way to be heard of, and it compounds.
- By the time I have an offer, the audience should already trust the thinking.

## How it works for me

- This digital garden is the engine. Notes become LinkedIn posts, posts become conversations, conversations become clients.
- Write about "why new hires take 3 months to ramp" and "why your AI agent keeps wrong answers." Same root cause, same buyer. See [[Knowledge management as a service]].
- No polish required. Gardens are expected to be messy.

## The risk

Content without a point of view is noise. Every piece should take a position, even a small one. "Here's what I think and why" beats "here's a list of tips."

## The metric

Not followers. Conversations. If a note starts a real exchange with someone who has the problem, it worked.
