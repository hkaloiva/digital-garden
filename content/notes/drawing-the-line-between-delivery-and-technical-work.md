# Drawing the line between delivery and technical work

My stated weakness: **"To be able to draw the line and not move into the technical part, however tempting."**

I like solving problems myself and not relying on anyone. That's a strength that becomes a liability at scale.

## The pattern

1. Work isn't getting done fast enough or well enough.
2. I step in and do it myself.
3. Short-term: problem solved.
4. Long-term: the team never learns, I become the bottleneck, and I burn out.

## The fix (in progress)

- Define what "done" looks like before touching it.
- Ask: "Am I the only one who can do this, or am I just the fastest right now?"
- If it's the latter, teach, don't do.
- Track how many times I intervene per week. If it's rising, something's broken upstream.
