# Prompt libraries as a product

A reusable prompt library is a real asset: battle-tested prompts tailored to a brand voice, campaign, and conversion goal. It's what turns mediocre AI output into marketing assets.

## The idea

- Most teams burn money on AI subscriptions and get inconsistent garbage.
- Courses teach prompt basics. Consultants give generic advice.
- The gap: **strategic context** — prompts that know your brand, your audience, your goals.

## The service shape

- Start with a "prompt audit" (free lead magnet) exposing where current prompts fail.
- Deliver organized libraries optimized for specific channels and goals.
- $499–1,999/month recurring. Saves 15+ hours/week of prompt tweaking.

## Why it fits a Dealmaker

Relationship-sold. You're the trusted advisor who understands their business, not a SaaS login. Recurring revenue + high-touch = compound trust.
