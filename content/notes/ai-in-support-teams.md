# AI in support teams

The interesting question isn't "should we use AI in support?" — it's "is our knowledge base ready for it?"

## The failure mode

Most companies bolt an AI agent onto a broken KB and wonder why it hallucinates. The AI isn't the problem. The **data layer** is. Garbage in, confident garbage out.

## Two linked pains, one root cause

See [[Knowledge management as a service]] for the full breakdown. Summary:

- **Pain A (human):** new hires take months to ramp because they can't find answers.
- **Pain B (AI):** the AI agent gives wrong answers because it retrieves from the same broken docs.

Fix the KB → both pains shrink.

## What I'm doing about it

This is the direction I'm exploring for my own business. Service first (audits), then productize (templates/checklists), then possibly software once the recurring problem is known. See [[Interesting problems that help many people]].
