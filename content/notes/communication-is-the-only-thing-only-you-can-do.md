# Communication is the only thing only you can do

In my role, the one thing that genuinely only I can do is **communication with the client and the feedback loop**. Everything else can, in principle, be delegated or systematized.

## Why this matters

- If I'm the only one who talks to the client, I'm also the bottleneck and the single point of failure.
- The goal isn't to hoard that role — it's to make it so good that the relationship compounds, while building systems so the team can deliver without me in every conversation.
- "They haven't misled me" — I protect my people. But the cost is that I stop giving them the quality work.

## The trap

Being the communication hub feels like importance. It's actually a ceiling. The SDM who never builds a team stays a glorified helpdesk forever. See [[Drawing the line between delivery and technical work]].
