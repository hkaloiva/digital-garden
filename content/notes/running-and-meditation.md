# Running and meditation

I find time for running and meditation most days. Not because I'm disciplined in the abstract — because without them, the rest of the system degrades.

## What they do

- Running burns off the stress that would otherwise leak into decisions and relationships.
- Meditation keeps me from reacting on autopilot — useful when a client concern lands and my first instinct is to explain why it doesn't matter.
- Together they create a gap between stimulus and response. That gap is where judgment lives.

## The honesty

I'm not an athlete and I'm not a monk. Some weeks are better than others. The point isn't perfection; it's that I notice when I stop, and I restart.

## The link to work

Service delivery is a stress-heavy job. If I don't process it somewhere, it comes out in the wrong place — at home, or in a sharp reply to a client. Running and meditation are where I process it.
