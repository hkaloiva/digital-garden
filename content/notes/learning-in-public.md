# Learning in public

The idea: share what you're learning while you're learning it, not after you've mastered it.

## Why

- Forces clarity — you can't explain what you don't understand.
- Builds an audience before you have a product.
- Creates a trail of thinking that compounds.
- Attracts people with the same problems.

## How (for me)

- This digital garden is the vehicle.
- Write notes, link them, publish. No polish required.
- Share on LinkedIn when a note matures into something useful.

## The risk

- Publishing half-formed ideas can look sloppy.
- Mitigation: frame notes as "thinking in progress," not "expert advice." Gardens are expected to be messy.
