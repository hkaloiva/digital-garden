# Why AI support agents give wrong answers

The AI isn't broken. The **knowledge base** it retrieves from is.

## The chain

1. KB is scattered, contradictory, and written for humans.
2. AI agent retrieves chunks from that KB.
3. Chunks conflict or don't answer the question.
4. Agent confidently produces a wrong or partial answer.
5. Customer trust erodes. Agent gets blamed. KB never gets fixed.

## The trap

Blaming the AI vendor. Buying a "better" model won't help if the source data is broken. The fix is upstream: [[Knowledge management as a service]].
