# Household is not my strength

Honest blind spot: at home, the household commitments don't interest me. My wife tells me to take the rubbish out and I don't do it. I'm responsible in the eyes of the client; at home, less so.

## Why I'm writing this down

- A blind spot I don't name becomes a cost someone else pays. Usually my wife.
- The contrast is stark: I keep promises to clients religiously and let small ones at home slide. That's not a strength; it's a misallocation.
- Naming it is the first step. Fixing it is the work.

## What I'm trying

- Treat small household tasks like SLAs: named, owned, with a time.
- Ask rather than wait to be told. Initiative, not compliance.
- Protect the evening ritual (bedtime story) as the anchor, then build outward.

## The risk

If I only fix this when it becomes a crisis, I've already failed. The goal is steady, boring improvement, not a grand gesture.
