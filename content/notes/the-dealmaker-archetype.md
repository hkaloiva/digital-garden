# The Dealmaker archetype

My founder archetype: **The Dealmaker** (primary), with **The Oracle** (secondary).

## What it means

- My leverage is my **network and presence**. I'm the trusted face, the connector, the person clients want to sit across from.
- Best-fit businesses: high-ticket consulting, advisory services, partnership brokering, client-facing professional services.
- Relationships are the moat. Anonymous volume plays are the trap.

## The blind spot

Dependence on me. When I'm the deal, what happens when I'm unavailable, burnt out, or want to work from a beach? The Dealmaker who never builds systems around their relationships hits a ceiling.

## The Oracle dimension

I don't just work my network transactionally — I see where things are going and position accordingly. Influence and impact in equal measure, not just wealth.

## The watch-out

Under stress, Dealmakers chase volume plays (marketplaces, anonymous SaaS) that strip out the relationship leverage they actually have. If the business doesn't let me be the deal, it'll feel hollow and stall.
