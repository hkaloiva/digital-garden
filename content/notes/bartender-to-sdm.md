# Bartender to SDM

I didn't start in IT. I started at the bottom — bartender on low pay in Burgas, penniless, wasting time, bad habits, a dream of a poker career. In 2015 I was bored with myself and had hit bottom. I left for England with friends, money from my mother, the rest kept as a buffer for the first months.

## The arc

- Solar panels installation — physical work, learning to show up.
- Dyson as a Customer Service Agent — "I always wanted to work in an office/corporate job."
- Internal move to Service Desk Analyst — the interview where I showed the Pravets photo.
- Today: Service Delivery Manager — one client, 100 employees, the whole infrastructure, ~95% Azure/Windows.

## What each step gave me

- Bartending: reading people, staying calm under pressure, service as a craft.
- Solar: showing up and doing the unglamorous work.
- Dyson: the systems of customer service — tickets, SLAs, feedback loops.
- SDM: ownership of an account, communication, and trust.

## The lesson I keep

The move wasn't talent or luck. It was movement — leaving a place where I was wasting time, keeping a buffer, and saying yes to the next unglamorous step. Competence compounded because I didn't skip steps.

## The risk now

I like solving problems myself and not relying on anyone. The same independence that got me here can trap me in the [[The hamster wheel|hamster wheel]] if I never let others carry weight. See [[Drawing the line between delivery and technical work]].
