# Ramp time and the new hire problem

A new support agent takes 3+ months to become productive. That's not a people problem — it's a **knowledge problem**.

## Why it takes so long

- The KB is written for people who already know the product.
- Tribal knowledge lives in senior agents' heads.
- Search returns 40 results, none of which answer the question.
- Asking a colleague interrupts two people.

## The cost

- Slow ramp = expensive onboarding
- Senior agents become human search engines
- Inconsistent answers reach customers
- New hires feel stupid and leave

## The fix direction

Audit the KB against real tickets. Find where people got stuck. Rewrite for retrieval, not for completeness. See [[Knowledge management as a service]].
