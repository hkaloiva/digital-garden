# AI as a hobby not just a tool

I use AI at work to make the job easier and to automate. But I also play with it — alongside work, as a hobby. That second part matters more than it looks.

## Why the hobby matters

- Tools are used for outcomes. Hobbies are where curiosity lives.
- The best ideas for the business came from playing, not from "applying AI to X."
- A hobby keeps the learning honest — no deadline, no deliverable, just interest.

## What I actually do

- Prompt engineering and context docs for my own setup (my me.md approach).
- Trying models and tools before they're "needed."
- Reading, tinkering, breaking things on purpose.

## The risk

Hobby can become procrastination — endless tinkering with no output. The check: does the play feed back into work or the business within a month? If not, it's just play. That's fine sometimes, but not always.
