# Reading List

Books, essays, and resources I'm reading or have read, with a one-line take. Updated as I go.

## Books

- *Deep Work* — Cal Newport — focus is a skill; protect it like a meeting.
- *The E-Myth Revisited* — Michael Gerber — work *on* the business, not just *in* it.
- *Atomic Habits* — James Clear — systems beat goals; identity beats systems.
- *Range* — David Epstein — generalists win in wicked domains.
- *The Mom Test* — Rob Fitzpatrick — talk about their life, not your idea.
